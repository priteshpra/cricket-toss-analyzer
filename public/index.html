Toss Intelligence Protocol
🎯 1. Statistical Consensus

Jab AI Historical Model aur Orbit/Betfair Exchange Volume dono same team ko favor karein — wahan statistical edge (~55%) banta hai.

⚠️ 2. Contrarian Load Trap Alert

Jab public retail punters kisi ek team par 75%+ heavy load daal dete hain, toh toss markets me aksar opposite underdog team jeetti hai ("Load Cut"). Aise matches me opposite team par value dekhein!

🔵 3. Steam vs Drift Movement
